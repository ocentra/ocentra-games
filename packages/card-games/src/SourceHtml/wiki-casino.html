# Content from https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casino_(card_game) Cassino (card game) Cassino, sometimes spelt Casino, is an English card game for two to four players using a standard, 52-card, French-suited pack. It is the only fishing game to have penetrated the English-speaking world. It is similar to the later Italian game of Scopa and is often said, without substantiation, to be of Italian origin. Cassino is still played today in Madeira, probably due to English influence. [... full Casino/Cassino article content ...]
