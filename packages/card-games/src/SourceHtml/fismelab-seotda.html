Seotda: Overview and the Differences with LA Seotda

Seotda (Sutda) is a traditional Korean card game played with two cards, where players compete by forming specific combinations to beat their opponent. The most notable winning hands are GwangTtaeng and Ttaeng, which are ranked based on the card combination. Seotda is a fast-paced game that combines both luck and strategy, making it a thrilling experience. LA Seotda is a modern variation of the traditional Seotda game. It retains the original rules and hand rankings but adds new elements and concepts, such as the Ttaeng Value, to create a more dynamic poker-like gameplay experience.

Basic Seotda Rules

Seotda is typically played with 20 cards from a Hwatu deck (used in Go-Stop), and the objective is to form the highest-ranking hand from two cards to defeat your opponent. Each card combination has a different rank, and the better your hand, the higher your chances of winning.

How the Game is Played:

  1. Card Distribution: Each player is dealt two cards.
  2. Betting Rounds: After receiving their cards, players can bet by choosing to Call, Raise, or Fold depending on the strength of their hand.
  3. Showdown: After the final betting round, players reveal their cards, and the one with the highest-ranking combination wins.

Main Seotda Hand Rankings

In Seotda, specific combinations of two cards form ranked hands:

  1. GwangTtaeng (광땡):

    • SamPal GwangTtaeng (3,8): This is the highest-ranked combination, composed of the 3 Gwang and 8 Gwang cards, beating all other hands.
    • IlPal GwangTtaeng (1,8): The second-highest hand, made up of the 1 Gwang and 8 Gwang.
    • IlSam GwangTtaeng (1,3): A combination of the 1 Gwang and 3 Gwang.

  2. Ttaeng (땡): A pair of the same number. Jang Ttaeng (10 Ttaeng) is the strongest, followed by 9 Ttaeng, 8 Ttaeng, and so on, down to 1 Ttaeng.

  3. Geut Hands (끗): Hands based on the sum of the two card values. Gap Oh (9 Geut) is the highest sum (9), followed by 8 Geut, 7 Geut, etc. If the sum exceeds 10, only the last digit is counted.

  4. MangTong (망통): The sum of the cards is 10, which doesn’t make any special combination.

Special Hands

There are also special hands that can override normal combinations under specific conditions:

  • 47 Amhaeng Eosa (암행어사): A combination of 4 and 7, which beats GwangTtaeng hands like IlPal or IlSam GwangTtaeng. Otherwise, it counts as a 1-Geut.
  • 37 Ttaeng Jabi (땡잡이): A combination of 3 and 7 that beats all Ttaeng hands (pairs) from 1 Ttaeng to 9 Ttaeng. If there are no Ttaeng hands, it counts as MangTong.

LA Seotda: A Modern Twist on Seotda

LA Seotda is a variation of Seotda that incorporates elements from poker, offering a more strategic gameplay experience. The introduction of the Ttaeng Value (extra winnings for strong hands) makes it an even more exciting and competitive game.

What is Ttaeng Value?

Ttaeng Value is a concept introduced in LA Seotda, where winning with certain strong hands grants you additional money from your opponents, much like a bonus in poker.

  1. How It Works:
    If a player wins with specific high-ranking hands like GwangTtaeng or Ttaeng, they receive extra winnings from the opponents who did not fold. This bonus amount depends on the hand ranking.

  2. Extra Winnings by Hand Type:

    • SamPal GwangTtaeng (삼팔광땡): 2x the winning amount.
    • IlPal/IlSam GwangTtaeng: 1x the winning amount.
    • Ttaeng Hands (장땡 to 1땡): 0.5x the winning amount.

  3. How It’s Displayed:
    After the final showdown, the additional winnings from Ttaeng Value will be shown on the result screen, displaying how much extra money each player earned or lost.

Differences Between Seotda and LA Seotda

  • Cards Used: Traditional Seotda uses 20 Hwatu cards, while LA Seotda uses a standard 52-card poker deck.
  • Hand Rankings: In traditional Seotda, the game revolves around GwangTtaeng and Ttaeng hands, but LA Seotda incorporates poker hand rankings and special hands to add more variety.
  • Ttaeng Value: LA Seotda introduces this concept, allowing players to earn extra winnings based on their hand’s strength.
  • Special Hands: LA Seotda includes poker-style hands like Four of a Kind or Straight Flush, which provide even more opportunities to win.

Seotda Strategy

  • Early Betting: In Seotda, the key to winning lies in betting strategy. If your hand is weak, it’s often better to fold early to minimize losses. However, if you have a strong hand like Ttaeng or higher, you should raise the bet to pressure your opponents.

  • Reading Opponents: Pay close attention to your opponents’ betting patterns. If they call or raise frequently, they may have a strong hand. Understanding their habits is essential for making the right decision.

  • Hand Ranking Knowledge: Knowing the special hands and how they work in different situations is crucial for maximizing your chances of winning.
