Card Games: War Group

In these games the players do not see their cards but hold them face down. At your turn you turn over your top card onto a discard pile. Under certain conditions you can win the pile, and the object is to collect the whole pack of cards. The children's games War and Beggar My Neighbour (also called "Beat your neighbour out of doors") belong to this group.

  • Camicia
  • Egyptian Ratscrew
  • Beggar My Neighbour
  • Slapjack
  • Snap
