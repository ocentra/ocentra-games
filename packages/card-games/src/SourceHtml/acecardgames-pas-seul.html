Pas Seul

A Klondike variant that only allows one pass through the deck. The name refers to a dance sequence for one person.

Build from Aces to Kings in suit on the empty stacks with circle (object stacks) in the upper right corner.

Click on the closed deck in the upper left corner to take a card and place it opened on the waste stack. Only the topmost card of the waste stack may be dragged. This card can be dragged to an object stack or to one of the six main stacks. On the main stacks you can build downward in alternating colors. A topmost card can be dragged to an object stack or one or more cards can be dragged from one to another main stack. Any card may be placed on top of an empty main stack.
