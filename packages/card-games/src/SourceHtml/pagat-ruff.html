Ruff and Honours

Ruff and Honours was an ancestor of Whist, played in England in the 17th century. A reconstruction can be found on the English Ruff and Honours page of the Medieval and Renaissance Games site.

The same site also contains a reconstruction of the closely related game Ruff and Trump

Ruff and Honours can be played online at TrapApps.
