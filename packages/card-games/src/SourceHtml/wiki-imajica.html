Imajica (card game)

Collectible card game

Imajica is a collectible card game based on the Imajica novel, and was published by Harper Prism in 1997.

Gameplay

[edit]

Imajica is a customizable collectible card game.

Publication history

[edit]

Inspired by the book, the Imajica Customizable Card Game (CCG) was released by Harper Prism in August 1997. The game was designed by Sean Curren and Hans Rueffert as a customizable card game for two or more players.[1][2] Barker planned on writing Imajica: The Forgotten Gospels inspired by the game, and Curren and Rueffert would use this book as the source for a 120-card expansion set In Ovo planned for release in March 1998.[3]

The game was released with a starter package that included two 60-card decks, and 15-card booster packs.[4]
