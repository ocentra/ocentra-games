How to play Lady Jane Solitaire(Lady Jane Solitaire Rules)

Images

Layout

(Double deck)

8 Foundation piles empty at the beginning.

10 Tableau piles. The first pile containing 1 card, the second pile 2 cards, the third pile 3 cards, and so on.

1 Stock pile with the remaining 24 cards face down.

1 Waste pile empty at the beginning.

Rules

OBJECTIVE

To move all the cards to the foundations.

FOUNDATIONS

Build up in suit, from Ace to King. Cards are moved individually.

TABLEAU PILES

Build down alternating colors. Cards are moved individually or in a group of cards if they are built down in suit. If a tableau pile becomes empty during play, any exposed card or grouped cards in sequence down in suit from another tableau pile may be moved into the empty stack.

STOCK PILE

Tap the stock pile to turn up 3 cards at one time from the stock pile to the waste pile. Only one redeal is allowed.

WASTE PILE

The top card is available for play onto the tableau piles or the foundations.

Tips and Tricks

Turn up the first cards from the stock pile before making any moves. This increases the game options so you can choose the best move.

Whenever possible, try to build the cards of the same suit. In this way, you can move the groups of cards from one tableau pile to another.

Try to empty the tableau piles as quickly as possible. Empty columns can be used as temporary storage to rearrange sequences of cards in suit and to place Kings or other cards that obstruct movements.

Try to remove cards from the longest tableau piles and from piles with low numbers covered with many cards above.

Move cards to foundations if it is possible. Those cards are no longer useful on the tableau piles. If it is necessary, you can move cards from the foundations to the tableau piles again.

As a general rule, do not deal new cards from the deck if you can still make moves in the tableau piles.
