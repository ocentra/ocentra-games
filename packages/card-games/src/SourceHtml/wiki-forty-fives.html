# Content from https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forty-fives # Fetched: Section F processing (manual) Forty-fives Forty-fives (also known as auction forty-fives, auction 120s, 120, and growl) is a [trick-taking card game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trick-taking_card_game) that originated in [Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ireland). The game is popular in many communities throughout [Atlantic Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlantic_Canada)([New Brunswick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Brunswick), [Newfoundland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newfoundland), [Nova Scotia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nova_Scotia), [Prince Edward Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince_Edward_Island)) as well as the [Gaspé Coast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gasp%C3%A9_Peninsula) in [Québec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qu%C3%A9bec). Forty-fives is also played in parts of [Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts) and southern [New Hampshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Hampshire) in [New England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_England), [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States), as well as in the [South Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Island) of [New Zealand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand). Some other very small populations of players also seem to reside in [Seattle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle), [Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_(state)) and [Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles), [California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California) by way of traveling from the east and spreading the game. There are several regional variations. Traditional Forty Fives goes to a score of 45 points, hence the name of the game. In the Auction Forty Fives variant the score goes to 120 points and requires bidding. In many areas outside of Canada, Auction Forty Fives is simply referred to as Forty Fives. Although the number 45 has no relevance to Auction Forty Fives, the name persisted.
