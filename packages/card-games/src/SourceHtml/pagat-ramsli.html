Ramsli

A Hungarian game related to the French game Rams. The Croatian game Raub is also similar.

A version of Ramsli played by the Donauschwaben - the German settlers on the banks of the Danube - in 18th and 19th century Hungary is described on Rick Heli's Card Games of the Donauschwaben page.
