Preferánsz

The game of Preference is played in somewhat different forms in many central and eastern European countries, including Russia, Austria and Croatia. The Hungarian form is called Preferánsz.

A version of Preferánsz played by the Donauschwaben - the German settlers on the banks of the Danube - in 18th and 19th century Hungary is described on Rick Heli's Card Games of the Donauschwaben page.
