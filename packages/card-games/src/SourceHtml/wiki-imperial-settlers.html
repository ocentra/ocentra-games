Imperial Settlers is a 2014 city-building themed card-driven strategy board game published by Polish company Portal Games [pl] and designed by Maciej Obszański and Ignacy Trzewiczek [pl].[1][2][3][4][5][6][7]

The game was inspired by the computer game series The Settlers that Trzewiczek played when he was young.[8]

It won the 2014 Golden Geek Best Solo Board Game, 2014 Board Game Quest Awards Best Card Game and 2015 Gra Roku Advanced Game of the Year Winner awards.
